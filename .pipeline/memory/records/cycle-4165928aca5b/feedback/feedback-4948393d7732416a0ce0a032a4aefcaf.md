# Delivery feedback

Problem: The compact tool group is still a persistent mini-waterfall instead of a live waterfall that collapses after completion, the collapse behavior is not configurable in Settings, and the approval Policy label still competes with the action category on one row.
Reproduce: Run a turn with several tool calls and observe the group during execution and after every tool has completed. Open Settings and look for a low-emphasis tool-collapse option. Open an approval request under Standard and inspect the placement and visual treatment of the Policy label.
Expected: When collapse is enabled, show the live tool waterfall while any item is queued or running, then replace the completed group with one summary row; expose the behavior as a restrained Settings toggle defaulting to enabled; place Policy on its own row as a fixed-width centered background badge sized for the longest of Safe, Standard, YOLO, and Auto.
Actual: The completed group keeps several compact child rows, Settings has no collapse option, and Standard remains right-aligned on the same row as the action category without a badge surface.
Context: M1 Stone review of vspi-v0-2-0-workflow-integration revision 6.
